## Gahere-Rere
The rhythmic thumping of drums and the rich smell of roasting spiced fish drifted through the woven reed walls of the stilt-cabana. Outside, the beach was ablaze with massive bonfires, casting wild, flickering shadows over the half-elves dancing in the bioluminescent surf.

"How about this," Zara’s low, melodious voice cut through the noise of the beach party as she consulted her meticulous notes. "Gahere-Rere is a massive archipelago situated slightly south of the equator. Navigators must be warned: visual confirmation of the tree line requires an additional seven days of grueling sailing. Furthermore, extreme magnetic instability renders compasses useless." She paused, her quill hovering. "Under a black sky, true navigators must read the deep currents. You steer by the bioluminescent jellyfish migrations, glowing rivers beneath the hull."

Kareem groaned, leaning back in his hammock until the ropes creaked, his eyes tracking a group of laughing Half-Elves carrying a crate of fermented fruit wine past their open window. "Zara, you of all people know sailors are superstitious bastards. And frankly, you're putting me to sleep. Nobody is going to leave port for that."

Zara arched a silver eyebrow, her green eyes flicking up as a sudden flare from a bonfire cast wild, dancing shadows across her parchment. "Nobody can actually get here if they starve to death underestimating the distance, or sink because they use the wrong tools."

"Maybe," Kareem conceded, taking a deep swig from his cup as the crowd outside erupted into a melodic, wave-rhythmic song. "But look out there! Tell them about the reward! We have to mention these welcoming, dazzling, beautiful people riding the waves! Tell them about diving for pirate gold in the Gahere ring! Tell them about hauling yourself up a massive vine and realizing the floating tree has ripped the earth up into the sky with it! The hallucinogenic fruits! The rumor of the Living Memory sitting at the center of it all!"

Zara dipped her quill, her tone entirely deadpan. "I am fairly certain 'The Living Memory' is just what happens when you eat too many of those hallucinogenic fruits. If their grandfather was a 'True Elf', they would not be that tanned."

Kareem barked a laugh that sounded like rolling thunder, spilling a few drops of his wine. "Even better! Give them the thrill, sis."

Zara looked at her brother, then out at the glowing surf and the colossal, shadowy wall of the jungle looming behind the festival. A rare, sharp smile touched her lips. "The skill..."

"...and the thrill," Kareem finished, his eyes twinkling. He leaned forward, setting his cup down. "Wait. This is it. Give me the quill."

*Rere is big. You just won't believe how vastly, hugely, mind-bogglingly big it is. You may think it's a long way through the desert to the Temple of the Sun, but it's nothing to Rere’s jungles. When your outlook on the ship calls land because they see canopy on the horizon, you still have about seven days to sail with favorable winds. So why go there at all? Why risk it? Well, let us tell you about the Half-Elves, the beaches, the food, the jungle and the ocean...*
